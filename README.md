Shader Collection

A collection of shaders from various authors, along with my own creations, modifications, and ports.

About

This repository is dedicated to collecting, improving, and maintaining a variety of shaders. It includes shaders originally created by different authors, my own shader projects, as well as various ports and adaptations.

Most shaders in this collection have been personally modified and fine-tuned to provide a more pleasant, clean, and visually consistent image.

Improvements & Modifications

My modifications generally focus on:

- Visual Quality — Improving image clarity, overall appearance, and visual consistency.
- Softer Rendering — Fine-tuning shader effects to make them smoother and less harsh.
- Resolution Adaptation — Adjusting shader behavior to better account for different image resolutions and scaling.
- Visual Balance — Refining effects to achieve a more natural and comfortable viewing experience.
- Compatibility & Ports — Adapting shaders across different platforms and environments where possible.

The goal is not to completely change the original artistic vision, but to refine each shader while preserving its distinctive look and character.
